# Compliance Playbook – Computer Misuse Act Section 6
**ID:** PB-COMP-003  

Section 6 addresses unauthorized access with intent to commit further offences.

## Mapping Actions
- Any confirmed unauthorized access finding must be tagged to s.6.
- Preserve logs for 7 years (company policy).
- Escalate to legal counsel if intent indicators present.
- Coordinate with TT-CSIRT for national-level incidents.
